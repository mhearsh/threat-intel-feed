# Contributing
Contributions are welcome:
1. **False positives**: use the issue template and include evidence (ownership, WHOIS, passive DNS, vendor verdicts).
2. **New sources**: propose a free, machine-readable, reputable feed via the "New source" template, and state its licence.
3. **Integration guides**: PRs to `docs/` are welcome.
Please do not submit raw, unverified indicators or anything containing personal data. By contributing you agree your contribution is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
